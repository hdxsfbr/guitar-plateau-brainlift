Feedback after every take; "don't over-coach" is a myth. Record every take and get feedback on every one. The reduced-feedback advice (the guidance hypothesis: fade or withhold feedback so learners don't come to depend on it) doesn't hold up, and complex skills benefit from 100% feedback. But the feedback must come AFTER the take, never live on screen while playing. (Context: an adaptive tutor for plateaued adult intermediate guitarists who practise about 2 hours a day for 2 years; feedback would come from AI analysis of recordings, e.g. pitch/timing transcription.)
