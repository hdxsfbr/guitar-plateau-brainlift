Hear it before you drill it. Plateaued intermediate guitarists usually have a hearing bottleneck, not a technique or theory bottleneck. So stop drilling scales and theory you can already name: no concept (a scale, arpeggio, chord quality, lick or progression) gets drilled until the learner can hear it and sing or play it back by ear. Scales and theory stay in the curriculum, and theory remains a core component; what changes is ORDER and GATING — the ear gates the drilling. This opposes the common theory-first, scales-first and "more technique hours" advice. (Context: an adaptive tutor for plateaued adult intermediate guitarists who practise about 2 hours a day for 2 years.)
