# Open questions

These come from the knowledge tree (`knowledge-tree.md`) as of 2026-10-07 (CT). Evidence IDs refer to `evidence.csv`. Each question notes why it matters for the tutor and what would answer it. A question with no evidence ID means nothing was found.

## Problem
1. **Do adult intermediate guitarists who practise about 2 h/day actually plateau, and on which dimensions?**
   - The only trajectory evidence is the caution-flagged vocal-jazz study (E049); the adult-amateur literature says this is under-studied (E050).
   - *Would answer it:* longitudinal recordings of adult intermediates scored on separate dimensions.
2. **How much of a plateau is practice quality versus fixed limits?**
   - The variance-explained debate is unresolved: 21% / 29.9% / 61% (E043–E045). The twin studies (E047, E048) use perceptual outcomes, not playing.
3. **What makes adults stop or stall: tutor-addressable factors such as difficulty with practicing, or life factors?**
   - Factors are known (E038) but not their weights for 2 h/day learners.
4. **Is 2 h/day sustainable without injury for guitarists?**
   - Problems are prevalent (E051, E056), but dose-response and preventive-break evidence for guitarists is missing (E053–E055).

## Current practice
5. **What do teachers of adult intermediate guitarists actually diagnose and assign, and how often do they agree?** No study was found (see the search-log gaps).
6. **Can software satisfy Ericsson's "individualized guidance by a qualified teacher" criterion (E046), or only support "purposeful" practice?**
7. **When do adults want direction versus autonomy?** The evidence is n=6 (E040), and one SRL worksheet did not raise efficacy (E039).

## Learning evidence
8. **Does interleaving help intermediate players, or only novices on discrimination tasks?**
   - Results are mixed (E012, E057–E060), and learners misjudge schedules (E013).
9. **Is less feedback better for complex musical skills?**
   - The guidance hypothesis (E023) is not supported meta-analytically (E026), and complex skills may favour frequent feedback (E024, E025).
   - Delayed retention and transfer data in music are close to absent (E032 tested within one session).
10. **Does real-time visual feedback on audio (pitch/timing) produce the dependency effect seen with violin bowing kinematics (E032, E033)?**
11. **Is a self-controlled feedback schedule worth building?** After bias correction the effect is near zero (E028).
12. **Does external focus help in music at all (E031)?** If not, cue wording can be chosen for clarity rather than focus.

## Alternatives
13. **Does adaptive sequencing beat a well-designed fixed curriculum for skill-building in music?**
    - Only one small, null-but-favourable study exists (E007), with no adult or intermediate players.
14. **Does adaptivity add anything over a good human teacher plus practice?**
    - ITS ≈ human tutoring and is not better (E001, E004). The real comparator for this learner is a weekly lesson.
15. **Which grain of adaptivity matters: step-level feedback, mastery gating (E005) or task selection?**
16. **Theory-first or aural-first for adult improvisers?** The evidence covers school students or small samples (E064, E066).

## Feasibility
17. **Does transcription accuracy on GuitarSet (E081, E084) hold for home recordings by intermediates: cheap mics, room noise, string noise, bends?**
18. **What feedback interface avoids the "cannot play while watching" problem (E033)?** Post-take summaries versus live display.
19. **Will adults keep up 2 h/day with a tutor?** Mastery gating can reduce completion (E006).

## Measurement
20. **Which guitar dimensions can be scored reliably enough to drive adaptation?**
    - Pitch and timing look feasible (E085, E086).
    - Problem localisation is weaker (E086), and expressive/global dimensions are unreliable even for humans (E016–E021).
21. **How many observations per skill per learner are needed before a learner-model estimate is reliable for guitar (E014, E015, E008)?** And is that achievable at 2 h/day?
22. **What is the inter-teacher agreement on identifying a learner's bottleneck?** This is the gold standard a diagnostic tutor would need. Not found.
23. **Are stage-appropriate criteria needed for intermediates (E022)?** No validated rubric exists for adult intermediate guitar.
