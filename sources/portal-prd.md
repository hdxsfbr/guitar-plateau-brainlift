## PRD

Identify and research a focused, specific problem in education (e.g., reading instruction, math practice, homework, assessment, or AI tutoring) for a defined learner group and setting, in order to build an evidence-based decision-making framework (a "BrainLift").

## Business context

Educators and decision-makers often lack a structured, evidence-based approach to evaluating instructional practices. This assignment builds a research methodology that combines expert sourcing, structured knowledge organization, and AI-assisted critical analysis to produce well-defended, falsifiable positions on education practices.

## Impact metrics

Quality and traceability of research to credible sources; strength and specificity of spiky POVs; rigor of AI pressure-testing; clarity of proposed next test to validate or refute each POV.

## Functional requirements

1. Define a 2-3 sentence purpose statement with problem, affected population, desired outcome, and decision scope with boundaries (grade level, subject, time, resources).
2. Identify at least 3 credible experts/sources with different perspectives, each with a link, relevance explanation, key finding, and limitation.
3. Build a knowledge tree covering Problem/Learners, Current Practice, Learning & Evidence, Alternatives, Feasibility, and Measurement branches, each with findings, sources, and open questions.
4. Develop two specific, defensible "spiky POVs" that challenge consensus, each with consensus, counterpoint, evidence/limits, and practical implication.
5. Pressure-test each POV using a specified AI prompt, record the strongest objection, and defend/narrow/revise the POV with a proposed small test.
